# Kindra: pitch deck outline

Audience: APC SHS homeroom advisers. Five slides, followed by the live demo and the ranking.
All names on slides and screenshots are fictional or anonymized.

## Slide 1 — Title

**Main message:** Kindra helps advisers spend less time on paperwork and act sooner on what students need.

- Kindra: APC SHS Advisory and Intervention
- What it does today, and what you decide it does next
- Presenter names, date

## Slide 2 — The problem

**Main message:** The documentation and follow-up work is taking time advisers need for students.

- Case notes are spread across notebooks, Excel and Word
- The work happens almost every day and spills into overtime
- Not every student can be accommodated in the time available
- Parents and the guidance counselor are sometimes told late
- Early action can change the outcome for a student

## Slide 3 — Kindra today (then the live demo)

**Main message:** Kindra already turns a conference into a reviewed case report with tracked follow-ups.

- Notes or a corrected transcript (English or Taglish) become a draft report with citations
- You confirm which student the report belongs to, even when several are mentioned
- Every action item has an owner and a status; the dashboard shows what is overdue
- Guardian phone changes are verified separately and logged
- Privacy: you see only your advisees; names are hidden from the AI; every action is audited

*Footnote on slide:* The demo uses fictional students. Parts shown in sample mode are labelled.

## Slide 4 — The next phase

**Main message:** These are the features we propose next; you choose the order.

- **Capture faster:** record the conference live
- **Act faster:** urgent alerts to the parent/guardian and guidance counselor; automatic reminders for due and overdue actions
- **Know what to do:** a response checklist for each type of concern, plus AI advice that you review
- **Records in and out:** add students from an Excel template, export to Excel, anonymized export for papers and reports
- **Secure and lasting:** sign in with your Microsoft account; keep notes and transcripts as long as policy allows

## Slide 5 — The ask

**Main message:** Rank the five themes so we build what helps you most first.

- Rank the five themes from 1 (most needed) to 5
- Tell us what counts as "urgent" for parent and counselor alerts
- Tell us which channel parents actually respond to
- We will confirm privacy and retention rules with the DPO before building

## Two-minute talk track

> **(Slide 1, 10 s)** We're here to show you Kindra and ask you to decide what it does next.
>
> **(Slide 2, 25 s)** Right now case notes live in notebooks, Excel and Word. You've told us the work happens almost daily, it pushes you into overtime, and some students wait because there isn't enough time. When something is urgent, parents and the guidance counselor can hear about it late, and sometimes minutes matter.
>
> **(Slide 3, 30 s)** Kindra already handles the paperwork side. You enter your notes, or a corrected transcript, even in Taglish, and it drafts the report with citations back to what was said. If two students come up, you choose whose record it goes to. Every follow-up gets an owner and a status. And only you can see your advisees, the AI never sees their names, and every action is logged. Let us show you. *(Live demo, see PROTOTYPE_PLAN.md.)*
>
> **(Slide 4, 35 s)** Here's what we propose next. Recording the conference live. Alerting parents and the guidance counselor quickly when something is urgent, and reminding owners when actions come due. A checklist for each type of concern, with AI advice that you review and decide on. Excel import and export, plus anonymized copies for reports. And sign-in with your school Microsoft account.
>
> **(Slide 5, 20 s)** We can't build it all at once, so we need you to rank these five themes. We also need two answers from you: what counts as urgent, and how parents are best reached. We won't claim time savings yet; we'll measure that once you're using it.
